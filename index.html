What The Allies Is

It has to be both.

The Allies is a community and a movement. One faces inward, to the men in it. The other faces outward, to the young men watching.

A Community

For the men in it.

Men showing up for men — as friends, and as a community, helping one another in their personal and professional lives. Real connection, built in person, with people who know your name.

A Movement

For the young men watching.

An antidote to the manosphere — the online world teaching boys a hostile version of manhood — met with something better: grown men modeling healthy behavior in plain sight.

Where It Started

It started with a question.

"How can I help you?"

In August 2023, Kelly Slattery launched Power Ladies from a Venice garage with 35 women and one intention: build a community of people who genuinely help one another. Today the Power Allies network is more than 1,300 members across 12 global chapters — and The Allies brings that same spirit to men.

The question that anchors every community in the network — and the ethos of Active Helping behind it.

Why Now

The crisis underneath the crisis.

Young men are more isolated than any generation on record — and connection is the mechanism that turns it around.

3% → 15%

Men reporting no close friends, 1990 to today.

~80%

Share of U.S. suicide deaths that are men — half the population (CDC).

3 in 4

Boys aged 11–17 who encounter manosphere content online.

The Allies answers with presence, not lectures: real men, in real rooms, showing what healthy looks like.

The Athlete Ambassadors

Men young men already look up to.

The Athlete Ambassador program brings together accomplished athletes who model emotionally intelligent masculinity — men who lead with connection, accountability, and service, both on and off the field. When they show up and demonstrate that strength and emotional openness belong together, it changes what's possible.

Connection

Leading with relationship, not dominance — friendship as strength.

Accountability

Owning the standard, on the field and off it.

Service

Showing up for others — the heart of the network's ethos.
